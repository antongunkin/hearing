# costco.com

Costco's US hearing aid center page lists Philips, Jabra Enhance, Rexton and Sonite aids sold through Costco warehouses, and the Audien OTC models are sold online. The report and pages show no Kirkland Signature hearing aid, and the config's model pattern matches none of the models seen. [1] [2]

## What this site is and whose

- The site is the Costco Wholesale US retail site. Its page 'Costco Hearing Aid Center' sells hearing aids and accessories. [1]
- The footer reads '© 1998 — 2026 Costco Wholesale Corporation'. [1]
- The hearing aid center page lists four hearing aid products: Philips HearLink 9060 miniRITE & Charger Plus, Jabra Enhance Pro 30 Micro RIE & Premium Charger, Rexton Reach RIC & Travel Charger, and Sonite Rise AI Smart RIC + Charger Go. These are the Philips, Jabra Enhance, Rexton and Sonite brands, not Kirkland Signature. [1]
- The center page says a free hearing test is included for members 18 or older. It says free service appointments are included for aids bought from the Costco Hearing Aid Center. It says the aids are FSA and HSA eligible, and that prices vary by state. [1]
- Costco also sells Audien Atom X and Audien Ion IQ OTC hearing aid bundles online only. Both appear under Health & Personal Care / Hearing Care Products / Assisted Hearing Aids. [2] [3]
- Kirkland Signature appears on the pages only as a corporate 'About Us' footer link. No page shows a Kirkland Signature hearing aid. [1]

## Structure

- The pages show two product URL forms: /p/-/{slug}/{itemId} and /{slug}.product.{itemId}.html. Both appear for the Audien models. [2]
- The sitemap index is listed in robots.txt. It points to sitemap_lw_* files such as _r_, _p_, _p_mod_, _l_, _c_, _s_, _i_ and _ac_. The 14 URLs counted include hearing-related product pages. [4] [5] [6]
- The sitemap hearing URLs include the Apple AirPods Pro 3 (hearing aid feature), the Audien Atom X and Ion IQ bundles, 'costco-hearing-aid-center.product.100476358.html', and HearLink AudioClip, remote control and TV adapter accessories for Philips hearing aids. [5]
- The Audien model pages show an item number and a model number, for example 'Item 2102381 | Model ATXCB001'. The URL id (4201049668) is different from the item number. [2]

## Rendering and quirks

- The hearing aid center page and both Audien pages are flagged needsRender false. Their text content shows without scripts. [1] [2] [3]
- The center page prices show split text, for example '$1,699' then '99'. Parse the cents separately. [1]
- The page text shows prices only for the four center aids. Several prices are 'Starting at' and vary by state. [1]
- robots.txt disallows URLs with ?*sortBy=* and ?*pageSize=*. It allows /*?currentPage=*pageSize=*. [4]
