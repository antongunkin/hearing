# sonova.com

Sonova's corporate group site (investors, news, careers, country sites) with no hearing aid catalog; products live on brand sites such as phonak.com, unitron.com, hansaton.com and advancedbionics.com. [1] [2]

## What this site is and whose

- Sonova International is the corporate site of Sonova, a hearing care group headquartered in Stäfa, Switzerland, founded in 1947. [1] [3]
- Sonova operates through the brands Phonak, Unitron, AudioNova and Advanced Bionics, plus regional brands such as Hansaton. [1] [3]
- The brands page lists Wholesale (Phonak, Unitron, Hansaton), Retail (AudioNova and other regional brands), Cochlear Implants (Advanced Bionics) and Consumer Hearing (Sennheiser). [2]
- In March 2026 Sonova decided to divest its Consumer Hearing business. [3]

## Structure

- Top-level sections are Investors, Innovation, Hearing Health, Our Brands, Who We Are (Our Company), News and Careers. [1] [2]
- Country sites sit under path prefixes such as /canada/, /usa/, /germany/ and /uk/, and /de/ holds the German version of the international site. Each country has its own sitemap_index.xml listed in robots.txt. [4] [5] [6]
- Other templates are /people/{slug}, /event/{slug} and /category/{slug}. Paginated lists use ?query-1-page=N. [7] [8]
- robots.txt has one group for all agents with no allow or disallow rules, and it lists 27 sitemap indexes. [4]

## Catalog, support, documents and apps

- The site has no hearing aid model catalog. The brands page only links out to the brand sites. [2] [1]
- Product news is under /category/product/ and covers Phonak launches such as Roger On Sphere and the EON portfolio, and Advanced Bionics news. [7]
- No user guides or datasheets were found. The PDFs are annual and financial reports, ESG reports, codes of conduct, tax strategies, AGM documents and an infographic. [9] [10] [11]
- The report shows no iOS or Android app links on any page. [1]

## Rendering and quirks

- Pages are server-rendered, with needsRender false on every page checked. [1] [2]
- The brands page shows a country filter and pagination (1, 2, 3 … 6) for AudioNova entities, so the retail brand list may load beyond the first page. [2]
- The report lists no failures. [1]
- The sitemap section of the report shows urlCount 0 and no sections, although 20 sitemap indexes were fetched. [12]

- The brands page links to phonak.com, unitron.com, hansaton.com, advancedbionics.com and sennheiser-hearing.com, and to regional retail brand sites such as audionova.dk, audionovaitalia.it and audiumbrasil.com.br. [2]
