# gn.com

gn.com is the corporate site of GN Store Nord A/S (investor, newsroom, careers); it has no hearing aid catalog, only brand links, and states the Hearing business is being sold to Amplifon. [1] [2]

## What this site is and whose

- gn.com is the corporate site of GN Store Nord A/S, listed on Nasdaq Copenhagen (GN.CO). [1]
- GN-owned brands for medical grade hearing technology: ReSound, Jabra, Beltone, Interton, Danavox. [1]
- Other GN brands: BlueParrott, FalCom (professional collaboration/hearing protection), SteelSeries and KontrolFreek (gaming). [1]
- On March 16, 2026 GN agreed to sell its Hearing business to Amplifon S.p.A.; all regulatory approvals were obtained on Nov 06, 2026 and completion is expected by the end of the year. [2]

## Catalog, support, documents and apps

- No hearing aid model catalog on gn.com. /Solutions/Products lists only business segments (Hearing technology, Office, Collaboration, Contact center, Gaming, Hearing protection). [13]
- No support pages, user guides, datasheets or apps were found. All pages show empty iOS and Android app lists. [3] [13]
- PDFs are corporate only (annual and interim reports, tax policy, AGM papers, EMTN bond documents, committee charters) under /-/media/Files/, all hosted on gn.com. [14] [15] [16] [17]
- The document download page links to an EMTN Programme page behind a terms-of-access notice restricting access. [14] [17]
