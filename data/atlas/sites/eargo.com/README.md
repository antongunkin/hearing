# eargo.com

Eargo's own brand site, read only through Web Archive copies: the catalog page (/products-hearing-aids) came back empty, and the useful content is the /guides/ pages for Eargo 8, SE, LINK and 7, each with a Use & Care and a Tech & Regulatory PDF. [1] [2] [3]

## What this site is and whose

- eargo.com is the website of Eargo, a hearing aid brand; the footer reads Copyright 2026 Eargo Inc. [3]
- The 2018 home page sold Eargo Max, Eargo Plus and accessories, with an invisible, rechargeable device and financing. [4]
- The newest guides pages cover Eargo 8, Eargo SE, LINK by Eargo and Eargo 7. [3]

## Structure

- The report found no sitemap and no live robots.txt. Only an archived robots.txt (April 2026) was read. [5]
- URL templates seen: www.eargo.com/{slug}, eargo.com/{slug}, shop.eargo.com/{slug}, /hearing-health/{slug}, /guides/{slug}, /company/{slug}, /misc/{slug}. [3]
- Archived robots.txt allows /products-hearing-aids, /guides, /faq and /blog, and disallows /lp/, /register and /schedule, among others. [5]

## Catalog, support, documents and apps

- The catalog is at /products-hearing-aids. The archived copy of 2026-04-17 has no text in the report, so the model list there is unknown. [1]
- Support pages are /guides/ and /guides/eargo-8/, /guides/eargo-7/, /guides/eargo-se/ and /guides/link-by-eargo/. Each device page has Use & Care Guide and Tech & Regulatory PDF download links. [2] [6] [7] [8]
- The 2018 home page linked one user guide PDF, for Eargo Max, on a cloudfront host. [4]
- The Eargo 7 guides page mentions the Eargo Mobile App for iOS and Android. No store links were found. [6]
- shop.eargo.com has pages such as /eargo-5, /eargo-6 and /accessories. [3]

## Rendering and quirks

- The archived guides index is flagged as needing scripts, but its text was still readable. [3]
- The archived home pages of 2020, 2024 and 2025 had no links and were flagged as needing scripts. The 2025 copy has no text. [9] [10] [11]
- The archived /eargo-5 and /eargo-6 pages show only 'Redirecting to eargo.com…'. [12] [13]

## Links to other sites

- Outbound links go mostly to social networks (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube) and Google Fonts. The 2018 page also linked press sites (TechCrunch, GeekWire, hearinghealthmatters.org). [4]
- The only PDF host seen is d9jpa82qnx9c8.cloudfront.net (one file, 2018). [4]
- Old product pages /eargo-5 and /eargo-6 redirect to the home page. [12] [13]
