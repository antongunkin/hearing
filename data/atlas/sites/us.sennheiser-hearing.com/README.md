# us.sennheiser-hearing.com

US Shopify webstore of Sennheiser consumer hearing/audio (Sonova Consumer Hearing USA LLC); the only current hearing aid is the OTC All-Day Clear at /products/sennheiser-all-day-clear, with bundles and spare parts, mixed in with headphones and soundbars. [1] [2] [3]

## What this site is and whose

- Online store of Sennheiser US, operated by Sonova Consumer Hearing USA LLC. [1]
- Sells headphones, TV headphones, soundbars, hearing protection, spare parts and OTC hearing aids. [4] [5]
- The OTC hearing aid is All Day Clear, self-fitting, for perceived mild to moderate hearing loss; listed at $699.95 (was $899.95), 90-day trial, 1 year warranty. [2]
- The store is run on Shopify; the site has an agents.md describing UCP/MCP endpoints for shopping. [1]

## Structure

- Sitemap index at /sitemap.xml with products, pages, collections, blogs, articles and agentic discovery sitemaps; 1119 URLs. [6] [7] [8]
- URL templates: /products/{slug}, /collections/{slug}, /collections/{collection}/products/{slug}, /pages/{slug}, /blogs/{blog}/{slug}. [3] [9]
- The same product can appear under several collection-prefixed URLs (hearing, legacy-products, tv-hearing, hearing-spare-parts-and-accessories). [10] [11]
- robots.txt disallows cart, checkout, account, orders, and collection URLs with sort_by or filter combinations. [6]

## Catalog, support, documents and apps

- OTC hearing aid catalog page: /pages/otc-hearing-aids; collection of hearing items: /collections/hearing. [2] [5]
- All Day Clear model page: /products/sennheiser-all-day-clear; bundles: /collections/hearing/products/all-day-clear-power-pack-set and all-day-clear-additional-charging-case-set. [3] [10] [12]
- Discontinued hearing products are in /collections/legacy-products (All Day Clear Slim, Conversation Clear Plus); the legacy page marks Conversation Clear Plus as End of Life. [13] [11]
- All Day Clear parts (wax guards, ear tips, charging case, power pack, TV connector) are on the product page and in /collections/hearing-spare-parts-and-accessories. [3] [14]
- The All-Day Clear product page has a Downloads section listing languages (English among them), but the report finds no PDF links. [3]
- Support is at support.sennheiser-hearing.com (Help Center, Detailed Warranty information). [4]
- The All Day Clear app: iOS id 1632398035, Android com.sennheiser.alldayclear; Hearing Test app: iOS 1611165453, Android com.sennheiser.screener. [3] [15]
