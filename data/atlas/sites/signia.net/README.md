# signia.net

signia.net is Signia's official multi-locale brand site (WS Audiology); the en-us catalog lists models under /hearing-aids/ platform sections, and the config's catalog only covers the Integrated Xperience section, not the newer Multi-adaptive Xperience (MaX) models. [1] [2] [3]

## What this site is and whose

- Official Signia hearing aid website, with a footer crediting WS Audiology A/S. [1]
- The site is split into many locale sections (en-us, en-gb, de-de, etc.); the sitemaps list 2959 URLs. [4]

## Structure

- Platform sections under /hearing-aids/ in en-us: integrated-xperience, multi-adaptive-xperience, augmented-xperience, signia-xperience. [1] [2]
- Model URL template: /{locale}/hearing-aids/{platform}/{model}/, e.g. integrated-xperience/active-ix/, cros-ix/, insio-charge-go-ix/. [5] [6] [7]
- Each locale has its own sitemap (sitemap-{locale}.xml) and a shops-sitemap; robots.txt lists them. [4] [8]
- Locale paths differ in language: da-dk uses /hoere-apparater/, de-de /hoergeraete/, es /audifonos/, cs-cz /sluchadla/, while en-*, es-ar, de-ch use /hearing-aids/. [9] [10] [11]
- The en-us sitemap carries hreflang alternates linking each page to its other locales. [8]

## Catalog, support, documents and apps

- The en-us catalog overview is /en-us/hearing-aids/; the Integrated Xperience listing is /en-us/hearing-aids/integrated-xperience/, which lists models such as Insio IX and Insio Charge&Go IX. [2] [12]
- The Augmented Xperience listing names Styletto AX, Pure Charge&Go T AX, Insio Charge&Go AX, Pure 312 AX and CROS variants. [13]
- The Multi-adaptive Xperience page presents the MaX platform; the home page promotes Pure C&G MaX. [3] [1]
- Model support pages are at /en-us/support/hearing-aids/, listing Pure, Styletto, Motion, Silk, Active, Insio, Intuis and Orion; it links to a document portal for user guides and safety instructions, whose URL is not in the data. [14]
- The Signia App page lists iOS app id 1316911473 and Android com.signia.rta; the ar and da-dk hearing-aids pages also list iOS 6759666876 and Android com.wsa.henosis.signia (Signia One). [15] [16] [9]
- PDFs found are on cdn.signia.net: app quick guide, Signia Assistant leaflet, Signia One app user guide, a Motion X brochure and a UK modern slavery statement. [15] [17] [18] [19]

## Rendering and quirks

- Most crawled pages need no scripts; the support index pages (en-us, es-ar, ar, da-dk) and the Pure Charge&Go MaX model pages (en-us, ar, da-dk) are flagged needsRender. [20] [21] [22] [23] [24] [25] [26]
- Page text for en-us support includes a repair-request form protected by reCAPTCHA. [20]
- robots.txt disallows only the 'Opensolr Web Crawler' entirely and /sitecore/ paths for others. [4]

## Links to other sites

- Pages link to signia-pro.com (professional site), plus social networks; one footer link goes to careers.wsa.com. [1] [27]
- Documents are hosted on cdn.signia.net; one Danish repair form is on cdn.shopify.com. [15] [23]
- Other external links: wsa.showpad.com, signia-library.com, shoeboxonline.com, qrfy.io. [15] [28] [29]
- The report records no redirects. [1]

## Web Archive

- Web Archive has copies for /en-us (latest 2026-10-19), /en, /en-nz, /en-sg, /en-au and /en-in, with the most snapshots in 2021 and 2024 for en-us. [30]
