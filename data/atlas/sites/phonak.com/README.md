# phonak.com

Phonak's live, multi-locale brand site (Sonova brand) with model pages under /hearing-devices/hearing-aids and datasheets and user guides under /support; the en-us catalog and support pages are readable as plain page text. [1] [2]

## What this site is and whose

- Phonak is a hearing aid brand site; the footer reads 'Phonak – A Sonova brand'. [3]
- The site sells hearing aids, accessories, Roger microphones, earplugs and apps, and has a professional section. [1]

## Structure

- The sitemaps list 9322 URLs across locale prefixes such as /en-us (690 URLs), /en-uk, /en-int and /de-de. [4]
- The en-us model URL template is /en-us/hearing-devices/hearing-aids/{slug}, with 27 URLs, for example audeo-eon, audeo-eon-sphere and audeo-fit. [5]
- A product-comparison page sits under the same path and is not a model page. [6]
- Other locales use translated paths, such as /de-de/hoerloesungen/hoersysteme/{slug} and /fr-fr/systemes-auditifs/aides-auditives/{slug}. [4]
- The en-us knowledge base has 285 articles at /en-us/support/knowledge-base/articles/{slug}. [7]
- robots.txt lists a sitemap per locale, including https://www.phonak.com/en-us/sitemap.xml. [4]

## Catalog, support, documents and apps

- The catalog is https://www.phonak.com/en-us/hearing-devices/hearing-aids, with a side-by-side comparison page. [1] [6]
- Datasheets are at /en-us/support/datasheets, organised by Infinio, Lumity, Paradise, Marvel, Belong and Lyric platform tabs. [2]
- User guides are split by page: /support/user-guides/infinio, /eon, /accessories, /apps and /chargers. [8] [9] [3] [10] [11]
- The myPhonak app page is /en-us/hearing-devices/apps/myphonak, with a user guide archive back to version 4.0.4. [12]
- The phone compatibility checker is at /en-us/support/phone-compatibility. [13]

## Rendering and quirks

- The catalog, support, user guide and datasheet pages return their content as text without scripts, though the text is cut off in places. [1] [2]
- The document pages show 'View' and 'Share' buttons with no PDF URL in the page text. [8]
- robots.txt disallows /resources/ and various confirmation pages for all user agents. [4]
