# rexton.com

Rexton is WS Audiology's hearing aid brand site, with about 18 locale sections. The en-us catalog is the Reach family under /en-us/hearing-aids/reach/. Model pages carry PDF feature overviews on cdn.rexton.com, and most pages work without scripts. [1] [2]

## What this site is and whose

- Rexton brand website; the footer says © 2026, WS Audiology A/S. [3]
- The site offers Reach, BiCore, MyCore, TruCore and MotionCore technology families with BTE, RIC, instant-fit and custom styles. [4] [5] [6] [7] [8]

## Structure

- Locale-prefixed sections: /en-us, /en, /en-gb, /en-ca, /en-in, /en-za, /de-de, /da-dk, /fr-fr, /it-it, /es, /es-mx, /pt-pt, /pt-br, /nl-nl, /tr-tr, /ko-kr, /ja-jp. The sitemap holds 1480 URLs, 81 of them under /en-us. [9] [10]
- One sitemap per locale is listed in robots.txt, plus a German shops sitemap at /de-de/shops-sitemap/. The en-us sitemap lists hreflang alternates for other locales. [9] [10]
- en-us templates: /en-us/hearing-aids/{family}/{model}/ (reach, bicore, mycore, trucore), /en-us/support/hearing-aids/{type}/, /en-us/support/{topic}/, /en-us/professional/{slug}/, /en-us/hearing-health/{slug}/. [1] [11] [4] [5] [6]
- Non-English locales use localized path words, for example /fr-fr/aides-auditives/, /it-it/apparecchi-acustici/, /es/audifonos/, /pt-pt/aparelhos-auditivos/, /tr-tr/isitme-cihazlari/. [12] [13] [14] [15] [16]
- Mixed-case locale URLs such as /en-US/hearing-aids/reach also appear in the sitemap templates. [17]

## Catalog, support, documents and apps

- The en-us catalog is /en-us/hearing-aids/reach/. Model pages found: r-plus, rugged, r-li, custom, inox-cic-li. [1] [18] [19] [3] [20] [21]
- The global /en/hearing-aids/reach/ lists more models than en-us, including B-Li and Custom CIC-Li, with pages at /en/hearing-aids/reach/b-li/ and /en/hearing-aids/reach/custom-cic-li/. [2] [22] [23]
- Older families sit under en-us at /hearing-aids/bicore/, /hearing-aids/mycore/ and /hearing-aids/trucore/. BiCore models include custom-li, r-312, r-li and b. [4] [5] [6]
- Support pages: /en-us/support/hearing-aids/bte-rechargeable/ and other types, /en-us/support/faq/, /en-us/support/app/. The BTE support page points to a Document Library for user guides. [11] [24] [10]
- Documents are PDFs on cdn.rexton.com: product catalogues and per-model feature overviews under /-/media/rexton/global/files/feature-tables/reach/. Feature overviews seen for r-plus, rugged, the Reach family, b-li and custom-cic-li. [18] [19] [1] [22] [23]
- Apps: Rexton app on iOS (id 1356550309) and Android (com.connexx.rta; also com.rexton.bxsmartremote), described at /en-us/hearing-aids/rexton-app/. [25]

## Rendering and quirks

- The en-us pages checked do not need scripts to show content. [17] [1] [18]
- Some pages are flagged as needing render: /de-de/, /ja-jp/, /da-dk/hearing-aids/ and /de-de/local/de-de/downloads/. [26] [27] [28] [29]
- The model pages checked have no JSON-LD. The en-us home page has a LocalBusiness block. [18] [17]

## Links to other sites

- The en-us home page links to shop-us.wsa.com (a WS Audiology shop). Other locales link to wsa.com, and en-ca links to costco.ca. [17] [30] [31]
- The locale picker lists the regional sites, all on rexton.com. No redirects were recorded. [2]
- The BiCore page contains a malformed Play Store link, http://https//play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.connexx.rta. [4]

## Web Archive

- Web Archive has copies of the section roots /en-us, /de-de, /en, /en-gb, /fr-fr and /pt-pt, with the latest capture on 2026-10-05. The /en-us root has captures from 2020 to 2026. [32]
