# starkey.com

Starkey's official site (Starkey Laboratories): active, server-rendered, with three AI model pages under /hearing-aids/ and manuals hosted on cdn.mediavalet.com and eifu.starkey.com. The sitemap.xml returned 404. [1] [2]

## What this site is and whose

- starkey.com is the consumer site of Starkey, a hearing aid brand; the footer reads © 2007-2026 Starkey Laboratories, Inc. [3]
- The executives page lists Bill Austin as Owner and Chairman and Brandon Sawalich as President and CEO. [3]
- The site sells through hearing professionals: it offers 'Find a provider' and 'Schedule an appointment', and has an Internet sales policy page. [4] [5] [6]

## Structure

- Current model lines are Omega AI+ (marked 'All new'), Edge AI and G Series AI (marked 'Enhanced'). [1]
- Model pages follow /hearing-aids/{name}-artificial-intelligence[-plus]-hearing-aids: omega-artificial-intelligence-plus-hearing-aids, edge-artificial-intelligence-hearing-aids, g-series-artificial-intelligence-hearing-aids. [7] [8] [9]
- Other /hearing-aids/ pages are topic pages, not models: rechargeable-hearing-aids, waterproof-hearing-aids, bluetooth-hearing-aids, apps, accessories, batteries, costs-and-financing, reviews. The report counts 22 pages under /hearing-aids/{slug}. [10] [11] [12]
- /hearing-aids/styles/{slug} has 7 style pages, e.g. behind-the-ear and completely-in-canal. [13]
- The robots.txt names http://www.starkey.com/sitemap.xml, but that URL returns HTTP 404, so no sitemap URLs were found. [14]
- robots.txt disallows /en/, /en-AU/, /en-CA/, /en-GB/, /en-NZ/, /fr-CA/, .aspx, .js, .css, /sitecore/, */thank-you and /pro-site-locator/ for all agents. [14]
- Other sections: /hearing-loss/*, /tinnitus/*, /support/*, /press/*, /blog/articles/{year}/{month}/{slug}, /about-starkey-hearing/executives/{slug}, /international/{slug}. [4]

## Catalog, support, documents and apps

- The catalog is https://www.starkey.com/hearing-aids, which links the three model lines and the style pages. [1]
- The operations manuals page https://www.starkey.com/support/operations-manuals links about 35 PDFs, plus the Climate Disclosures and ESG Report PDFs that every page links. It covers older families (Livio, Muse iQ, Halo, Z Series, 3 Series), chargers, SurfLink and apps. [2]
- Manual PDFs are hosted on cdn.mediavalet.com (220 PDF links in the report), under paths like /usil/starkeyhearingtech/.../Original/{name}.pdf. [2]
- The Bluetooth page links Auracast Assistant QuickTip and Flyer PDFs. The compatibility page links the StarLink Edge LE Adapter User Guide PDF. The tinnitus page links a Tinnitus Consumer Brochure PDF. [11] [15] [16]
- The eifu.starkey.com viewer serves manuals as pdf?groupDocNumber=... (16 URLs). The example BKLT3014-EE-SC is the Mini Turbo Charger Operations Manual. [17]
- Support pages: /support/get-help, /support/hearing-aid-warranty, /support/operations-manuals, /support/apps. Compatibility is at /compatibility. [18] [19] [20] [15]
- Apps: My Starkey, T2 Remote, SoundCheck Live and Relax (tinnitus) on /hearing-aids/apps. The support apps page lists My Starkey, Thrive and Hear Share. Store IDs are iOS 1549559068, 336938292, 6449296331 and 966601084; Android com.starkey.mystarkey.release, com.starkey.T2Remote.android, com.starkey.soundchecklive.release and com.starkey.tinnitus. [12] [20]
- Support phone numbers: 1-866-637-9243 for wearers and 1-800-211-7789 for veterans. [18]

## Rendering and quirks

- All fetched pages report needsRender false except /schedule-an-appointment, which needs scripts. [1] [5]
- The Omega AI+, Edge AI and G Series AI model pages show their text content without scripts. [7] [8] [9]
- No page has JSON-LD, except locations.starkey.com with LocalBusiness. [21] [1]
- Model pages show feature text such as battery life and waterproofing, but no prices on the pages shown. [7]
- One external PDF link carries a Cloudflare challenge token (__cf_chl_tk), so it is not a stable URL. [22]
