# audienhearing.com

Audien Hearing is a live Shopify store selling Audien-brand OTC hearing aids (Atom and Ion series) directly; /collections/all lists models mixed with accessories, open-box and duplicate variants, and no PDFs or apps were found. [1] [2]

## What this site is and whose

- The site is the Audien Hearing online store, selling OTC hearing aids, accessories and hearing-care products direct to consumers. [2] [3]
- Its pages are built on Shopify: the cdn.shopify.com image host and the sitemap_products_1.xml, sitemap_pages_1.xml and sitemap_collections_1.xml sitemap names show this. [4] [5]
- The home page lists the ATOM series (in-ear): Atom X (2nd Gen), Atom Air, Atom Pro 2, Atom One. It lists the ION series (behind-the-ear): Ion Pro 2 (tagged 'Last chance'), Ion IQ and Ion Air. [2]
- The site runs a Black Friday sale (up to $338 off bundles) and offers a 45-day risk-free trial. It also has Outlet/Refurbished, Trade-In and AudienCare programs. [2] [6] [7]
- The home page links to wollson.co, which could be a parent or sister company. The pages do not say. [2]

## Structure

- robots.txt lists one sitemap, /sitemap.xml. The sitemaps cover products, pages, collections, blogs, articles and metaobject pages, plus sitemap_agentic_discovery.xml. The report counts 449 URLs. [8] [5]
- sitemap_agentic_discovery.xml contains only /agents.md. [9]
- URL templates are /products/{slug} (68 seen), /collections/{slug}, /pages/{slug} (59), /blogs/{blog}/{slug} and /policies/{slug}. The blogs are home (180), common-questions (48), tinnitus (29), hearing-loss (25), hearing-aid-reviews (19), hearing-test (8) and advertorial (5). [5]
- /cart/{variant}:1 permalinks (51) appear as a template, but robots.txt disallows /cart/. [8]
- robots.txt disallows collection URLs with sort_by or '+' filters, /search is not disallowed, and /checkout, /account and /orders are blocked. [8]
- The store has an /agents.md file describing Shop skill and UCP integration. The UCP profile is at /.well-known/ucp and the MCP endpoint at /api/ucp/mcp. [10]

## Catalog, support, documents and apps

- The catalog is https://audienhearing.com/collections/all. It lists every product, with hearing aids mixed in with accessories, chargers, a protection plan, gift card, and open-box, 'Like New', 'Sale Price' and TTS variants. [1]
- The current model pages are /products/the-audien-atom-x-otc-hearing-aid, audien-atom-air-otc-hearing-aids, atom-pro-2, audien-atom-one-hearing-aid, audien-ion-pro-otc-hearing-aids, ion-iq and ion-air. All were fetched and carry Product JSON-LD. [11] [12] [13] [14] [15] [16] [17]
- /products/audien-atom is a legacy Atom page that still has a Product entry. The catalog lists several older and variant products (Atom Pro, Atom 2, Atom X Connect, Audien ion, 'Specialist Bundle', open-box), and the sitemap shows e.g. audien-ion-otc-hearing-aids-new and audien-atom-2-otc-hearing-aids-reworked. [18] [1] [4]
- Support is at /pages/support (call 1-800-400-HEAR, support@audienhearing.com, message form via app.sendhark.com) and a Zendesk help center at audienhearing.zendesk.com/hc/en-us. It has no PDF links. [19] [2] [20]
- No PDF documents (user guides, datasheets) were found on any fetched page. The report lists no PDF hosts. [2]
- No iOS or Android app links were found on any fetched page, although the shop mentions 'Find My Device'. [2]
- Collections include /collections/accessories, /collections/maintenance and /collections/best-otc-hearing-aids. [21] [22] [23]
- Retailer pages exist for Walgreens, Target and Walmart, with a Store Locator via stockist.co. The footer also lists Costco, CVS, BJ's, Staples and Giant Eagle. [24] [25] [26] [27] [1]

## Rendering and quirks

- All fetched pages have needsRender false. Product, catalog and navigation content is in the static text. [2] [1] [16]
- The report lists no fetch failures and no redirects. [2]
- The home page shows a countdown timer at 00 and a promotional banner. Prices shown on pages are sale prices that change during Black Friday (e.g. Atom Air $129/pair in 'Popular Products' versus $149 in the catalog). [2] [1]
- The Ion IQ page contains a trade-in/refurbished condition widget (BTE, EV1, EV3, Atom, Atom pro...). [16]
