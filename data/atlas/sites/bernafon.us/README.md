# bernafon.us

bernafon.us is the live US site of the Bernafon brand (Demant). It has a static catalog at /products/hearing-aids with model pages at /products/{n}/{slug}. PDFs sit on damcdn.demant.com and assets.ha.demant.com. [1] [2]

## What this site is and whose

- US consumer and professional site of the Bernafon hearing aid brand; the brand config lists group Demant. [3]
- Current families listed on the catalog page: Encanta, Alpha XT, Alpha. [1]
- Leox and Viron family pages exist (sitemap) but are not listed in the catalog; Declarations of Conformity also mention Zerena. They look like older families. [4] [5] [6]

## Structure

- robots.txt allows everything and lists https://www.bernafon.us/sitemap.xml, which has 121 URLs (support 66, products 25, for-professionals 12, about-us 9, hearing-loss 5). [7] [8]
- Model pages: /products/{n}/{slug}, e.g. 663068/encanta-minirite, 521423/alpha-xt-minirite-t-r, 521422/alpha-xt-minirite-t, 391550/alpha-minirite-t, 403917/cros-and-bicros-minirite-t-r. [9] [10] [11] [12] [13]
- Accessory pages: /products/accessories/{n}/{slug}; family pages: /products/hearing-aids/family/{slug}; category page: /products/hearing-aids/category/behind-the-ear. [14] [15] [16]
- Sitemap also lists sitecore/content/... paths (3 found) that mirror support pages. [8]

## Catalog, support, documents and apps

- Catalog: /products/hearing-aids lists 4 miniRITE models (Encanta miniRITE, Alpha XT miniRITE T R, Alpha XT miniRITE T, Alpha miniRITE T). [1]
- Support pages under /support: compatibility-guide, pairing, life-with-a-hearing-aid, bernafon-app, care-and-cleaning, batteries-and-charging, troubleshooting. [17] [18]
- Documents: the Resource center lists technical data, brochures, instructions for use and guides as PDFs. Model pages carry their own IFU and technical data PDFs. [2] [10]
- Compatibility guide links two PDF overviews (charger and CROS) on assets.ha.demant.com. [19]
- The App page lists app IFU PDFs; the Bernafon App is iOS id 1597062293 and Android com.bernafon.bernafonapp. [20] [21]

## Rendering and quirks

- Catalog /products/hearing-aids, model pages and the Resource center need no render per the report. [1] [9] [2]
- Several hub pages are flagged needsRender: /products, /support, /support/pairing, /products/accessories, /support/compatibility-guide, /hearing-loss and others. The accessories list shows no items in the plain fetch. [22] [23] [24] [19]

## Links to other sites

- Every page links to bernafon.com (59 links) and app.certexpress.com (59 links, tax exemption certificate). [3]
- PDFs are hosted on damcdn.demant.com (37) and assets.ha.demant.com (4, with rev and hash query strings). [2] [19]
- App store links go to apps.apple.com and play.google.com. No redirects were recorded. [20]
