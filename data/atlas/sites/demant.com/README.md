# demant.com

demant.com is the corporate site of the Demant group (investor relations, sustainability, news, careers). It has no hearing aid catalog, no model pages and no user guides, so it is not one brand's site. [1] [2]

## What this site is and whose

- Demant is a global hearing healthcare group with three business areas: Hearing Care, Hearing Aids and Diagnostics. [2]
- The site is the corporate site of Demant A/S, listed on Nasdaq Copenhagen. William Demant Foundation holds the majority of shares. [1] [3]
- The group's history names the hearing aid brands Oticon, Bernafon, Sonic and Philips. It also names the diagnostics brands Interacoustics, Maico, Amplivox and Grason-Stadler. [4]
- Latest news items mention Oticon Reveal, the Bernafon Encanta family, Philips HearLink and Sonic Radiant SE. They are press items only. [5]
- Demant completed the divestment of Oticon Medical in 2026 and the sale of EPOS to ACCO brands. [5]

## Structure

- The sitemap lists 253 URLs. Main sections are /about (105), /blog (74), /investor-relations (20), /jobs-and-career (11), /reports-2022 to /reports-2025 and /sustainability (7). [6]
- News articles follow https://www.demant.com/about/latest-news/{slug} (80 pages). [5]
- The graduate blog has posts under /blog/graduate-blog/posts/{slug} and authors under /blog/graduate-blog/authors/{slug}. [7]
- Management profiles follow /about/management-and-governance/{slug}. [8]
- robots.txt disallows /sitecore/ and /*/~/link.aspx* and lists https://www.demant.com/sitemap.xml. [9]

## Catalog, support, documents and apps

- No catalog or model pages exist on this site. [6] [2]
- No support pages, user guides or datasheets were found. All PDFs are corporate documents: annual and interim reports, governance, sustainability and capital markets day files. [3] [10]
- The report finds no app links on any page. [1]
- The corporate pages point to Oticon pages on oticon.com. [11]
- The Hearing Care business is Audika Group, a network of more than 3,500 clinics in 20+ countries. The site links to a clinic finder on audikagroup.com. [11]

- The report shows no JSON-LD on the pages it fetched. [1]

- The page titled 'Reports 2024' appears at /reports-2025. Several /sustainability/copy-of-* pages duplicate the originals. [16] [17]
