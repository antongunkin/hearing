# apple.com

Apple's AirPods Pro 3 section on apple.com: a consumer earbud with a Hearing Aid feature (FDA-authorized), served as static HTML with five pages and no model-per-URL catalog. [1] [2]

## What this site is and whose

- The site is Apple's own AirPods Pro 3 product section on apple.com. [1]
- AirPods Pro 3 is a consumer earbud with Hearing Test, Hearing Aid and Hearing Protection features, used with an iPhone or iPad. [2]
- The Hearing Aid feature has received FDA authorization. It is meant for people with perceived mild to moderate hearing loss. [2]

## Structure

- The section has five pages: overview, hearing-health, specs, compare and feature-availability. The report's template is https://www.apple.com/airpods-pro/{slug}. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]
- robots.txt lists sitemaps for shop, autopush, newsroom, retail and today. The report found 0 URLs in sitemaps, and none of them is specific to airpods-pro. [6] [7]

## Catalog, support, documents and apps

- The catalog entry is the overview page, which compares AirPods Pro 3 with AirPods 5 and links to all AirPods models. [1]
- The compare page lists AirPods 5, AirPods 4, AirPods Pro 3, AirPods Pro 2, AirPods Max, AirPods Max 2 and AirPods (2nd and 3rd generation), each with its own tech specs link. [4]
- The specs page holds the AirPods Pro 3 technical data and is the only model-level page in this section. [3]
- Documents are Apple environmental PDFs: the AirPods Pro 3 Product Environmental Report, the Regulated Substances Specification and the Environmental Progress Report 2026. No user guide or datasheet is linked. [3]
- The footnotes point to support.apple.com articles (120850, 120409, 123184) for Hearing Protection, cleaning and heart rate. The report does not list a support page. [2] [4]
- The pages link to one iOS app, id 375380948, and no Android app. [1]
